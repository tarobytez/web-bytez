## Hello! Welcome to my guide site! 

The purpose of this site is to provide any clients / groups I am working with a place to reference when adding to / managing their sites.

There are two main sections on the site currently. One covers the basics of the web, such as how to boost your content in search engines, how colours work on the web, and designing for different device sizes. The second has guides on editing your Wordpress site, specifically one created with the Astra theme and the Elementor block editor.

If you are someone seeing this site on my portfolio! Hi! This site was built with Astro, using their Starlight docs template. Take a peak around. If you have a job for me, contact me using the email in the header :)

If you have no idea who I am and are happening upon this site, do whatever you want! Learn something, scroll aimlessely, the world is your oyster!
